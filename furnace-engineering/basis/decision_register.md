# Decision register — SHV-FUR-6700 Rev A

The sources do not agree everywhere. This register records each decision this design makes, why, and where the evidence is. "C-xx" is a calculation in `out/calc/SHV-FUR-6700-CR.html`.

| # | Decision | Why | Sources weighed | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | **The kettle hangs from its rim flange.** It does not stand on the floor. The plant model's five kettle props are deleted. | Rev H's validation treats the kettle as suspended (76–78 t), and Rev K's levels leave 515 mm under the kettle. STL-EF Rev D gives a stress-checked load path for this exact envelope. The as-built SH-INS furnaces stand the kettle on the ground or on HD board. That is a different arrangement, and the reconciliation itself lists it as unknown. | Rev H, Rev K, STL-EF, SH-INS | C-12 |
| D-02 | **The rim flange is 120 × 40, continuous** (STL-EF). The SH-INS top closure is kept relative to the flange underside: a 50 × 12 lip plate 200 mm below it, 25 mm HD boards, 3 × 50 blanket, and 25 mm clear. | The flange is part of the load path. The SH-INS 25 × 200 "top plate" was a heat seal on a floor-standing kettle. | STL-EF, SH-INS-01..05 Rev B | C-12 |
| D-03 | **The cap beam is a fabricated box 300 × 100 × 10** on the casing top (+0.150 → +0.250). A 6 mm chequered sectional deck sits at +0.256, so the rim (+0.300) stands 44 mm proud as a spill lip. | This keeps Rev K's casing top and rim levels. STL-EF's SHS 300 × 150 used a lower rim datum. | Rev K, STL-EF | Drawing 003 |
| D-04 | **The hearth is built up as:** 10 plate + 2 × 25 CaSi + 25 microporous + 3 × 50 blanket 64 kg/m³ (A–B–A strips per SH-INS) + 25 HD board wear face. | D-05 | **The wall lining is 250 mm:** 175 folded module 128 kg/m³ 1430 grade, Z-anchored, + 50 microporous + 25 CaSi, with a 50 mm rebate above the HD-board seat. | Skin is 61–65 °C at a 900–1,000 °C hot face, against 75–81 °C for all-fibre 200 + 50. | D-06 | **Kettle corners are bent R250 inside / R300 outside.** The corner shields are rolled to R350. | SH-INS describes the shields as "curved round the corner". STL-EF uses R250. | SH-INS, STL-EF | — |
