# Decision register — SHV-FUR-6700 Rev A

The sources do not agree everywhere. This register records each decision this design makes, why, and where the evidence is. "C-xx" is a calculation in `out/calc/SHV-FUR-6700-CR.html`.

| # | Decision | Why | Sources weighed | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | **The kettle hangs from its rim flange.** It does not stand on the floor. The plant model's five kettle props are deleted. | Rev H's validation treats the kettle as suspended (76–78 t), and Rev K's levels leave 515 mm under the kettle. STL-EF Rev D gives a stress-checked load path for this exact envelope. The as-built SH-INS furnaces stand the kettle on the ground or on HD board. That is a different arrangement, and the reconciliation itself lists it as unknown. | Rev H, Rev K, STL-EF, SH-INS | C-12 |
| D-02 | **The rim flange is 120 × 40, continuous** (STL-EF). The SH-INS top closure is kept relative to the flange underside: a 50 × 12 lip plate 200 mm below it, 25 mm HD boards, 3 × 50 blanket, and 25 mm clear. | The flange is part of the load path. The SH-INS 25 × 200 "top plate" was a heat seal on a floor-standing kettle. | STL-EF, SH-INS-01..05 Rev B | C-12 |
| D-03 | **The cap beam is a fabricated box 300 × 100 × 10** on the casing top (+0.150 → +0.250). A 6 mm chequered sectional deck sits at +0.256, so the rim (+0.300) stands 44 mm proud as a spill lip. | This keeps Rev K's casing top and rim levels. STL-EF's SHS 300 × 150 used a lower rim datum. | Rev K, STL-EF | Drawing 003 |
| D-04 | **The hearth is built up as:** 10 plate + 2 × 25 CaSi + 25 microporous + 3 × 50 blanket 64 kg/m³ (A–B–A strips per SH-INS) + 25 HD board wear face. The hearth surface is at −1.315, leaving 255 mm under the kettle. | A suspended kettle puts hot gas on the floor. The SH-INS B3 blanket alone, laid on the pad, would take the RCC to ≈556 °C in steady state. | SH-INS (B3), STL-EF hearth | C-04 |
| D-05 | **The wall lining is 250 mm:** 175 folded module 128 kg/m³ 1430 grade, Z-anchored, + 50 microporous + 25 CaSi, with a 50 mm rebate above the HD-board seat. | Skin is 61–65 °C at a 900–1,000 °C hot face, against 75–81 °C for all-fibre 200 + 50. Wall loss falls by ≈40 %. Rev K's 53–55 °C target cannot be reached in 250 mm (O-08). | Rev K, SH-INS, STL-EF REF data | C-04 |
| D-06 | **Kettle corners are bent R250 inside / R300 outside.** The corner shields are rolled to R350. | SH-INS describes the shields as "curved round the corner". STL-EF uses R250. | SH-INS, STL-EF | — |
| D-07 | **Leak pits** (700 × 700 × 600, Rev K) sit on the kettle centreline at Y ±2,000 under the hearth. Each has a sacrificial 25 mm board over a 700 × 700 hearth opening, a steel chute through the grillage, and a locally thickened pad. | Rev K gives the size only. Under a suspended kettle, the hearth is where escaping zinc goes. | Rev K | Drawing 002 |
| D-08 | **Furnace pit:** RCC 200 retaining walls run along the casing long sides and join the burner pits. A 119 mm ventilated gap is left round the casing, with grating at FFL. | The casing is sunk 1.575 m. The plant model showed no wall between the casing and the soil. | Rev K, HDG | — |
| D-09 | **Two pit fans, 3 HP each,** one per burner pit, extracting at pit-floor level. | The source shows one fan. Both pits hold a gas train, and LPG is heavier than air. | CBA, PID-02 | C-11 |
| D-10 | **The flue runs in an internally lined steel duct:** 700 × 400 clear, lined with 100 module + 50 microporous inside 6 mm steel. It sits on supports in a vented RCC trench 1,260 wide. | Rev H's 230 IFB + 50 fibre trench gives RCC at 90–350 °C and a cover plate at ≈120 °C, and its 700 × 1,000 section runs at 2.2 m/s, slow enough for ash to settle. The duct runs at 5.4 m/s, with RCC ≈64 °C. | Rev H + 12 Oct validation | C-04, C-07 |
| D-11 | **The dryer clear chamber is 6,000 × 7,500 × 1,400.** | It takes the 7.4 m jig beam and the 7.6 m doors. Rev H's 7,000 would not. | Rev H, HDG, SH-RBR | — |
| D-12 | **The supply and extraction boxes are 1,300 × 350 × 7,000,** centre-fed and centre-drawn. Each face is 7,000 × 1,000, perforated Ø15 @ 37 staggered 60° (14.9 % open, ≈5,900 holes). | Hole ΔP / feed velocity pressure = 2.05 (the uniform-spread criterion is ≥ 2). Rev H's end feed gave 0.30. A 350 mm depth keeps the box face 400 mm from the P1 centreline. | Rev H validation, HDG | C-09 |
| D-13 | **Equipment around the furnace is re-placed:** combustion blower plinth at X +2.7…+4.1 m, pit fans east of each pit, gas skid at Y −7.6 m. | In the HDG layout the blower sat inside the 150 × 150 air ring and the pit fan sat on the blower. | HDG, PID-02 | 3D model |
| D-14 | **The dryer pit lining is 100 mm** ceramic-fibre board with a 1 mm aluminium liner. | RCC is 62 °C with 100 mm, against 77 °C with 50 mm. | — | C-04 |
| D-15 | **Ventilated foundation:** the casing sits on an ISMB 150 grillage at 600 c/c on the pad. Pad top is −1.725 and underside −2.325. The channels open into the pit gaps. | It keeps the pad top at ≤ 65 °C under a hot hearth. Rev K's pad (3,000 × 8,500 × 600) is kept, 150 mm lower. | Rev K, STL-EF (ventilated pedestals) | C-04 |
| D-16 | **Dryer top:** a 250 mm insulated kerb runs round the pit, with a fixed cover over P2–P3 flush with the kerb. The doors run on rails above both. | In the HDG model the doors and the fixed cover sat at the same height and would collide. | HDG, SH-RBR | 3D model |
| D-17 | **Casing 8,200 × 2,750 is an inside dimension.** The outside is 8,212 × 2,762 plus stiffeners. | This matches the SH-INS reconciliation (casing inner) and Rev H's 700 mm gap stack. | SH-INS, Rev H | — |
| D-18 | **Gas enters the skid at A1 on the east.** A11 sits at the west end nearest the furnace. | The run is shorter, with no doubling back. | PID-02 | C-06 |
| D-19 | **The recirculation blower is 250 mm further west** than in the HDG model. | The inlet spool must hold the 560 mm cruciform straightener (SH-MB-01: mandatory). | SH-MB-01 | — |
| D-20 | **The discharge trench sits at Y −7.55…−8.65 m.** | The 900 × 675 blower outlet then drops fully into it. | Rev H, HDG | — |
| D-21 | **Combustion air is set at λ = 1.125** (12.5 % excess air). | Rev H's 1,132 Nm³/h is λ ≈ 1.00, too lean for pulse-fired HV burners. Full fire at λ 1.125 needs ≈1,430 m³/h at 35 °C (O-04). | Rev H validation | C-01 |
