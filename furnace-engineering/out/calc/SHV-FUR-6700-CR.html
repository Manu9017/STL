C-04 · Linings: casing skin, hearth and foundation, flue duct and trench, dryer pit (1-D, k(T))

Method
  • Steady 1-D conduction through layers with k = a + b·T_mean, iterated to convergence.
  • Free surface: h = 1.42 (ΔT/1.2)^0.25 + ε σ (Ts⁴ − Ta⁴)/ΔT, ε = 0.85, Ta = 35 °C (STL-EF method).
  • Ventilated foundation: casing underside to 50 °C channel air, h = 12 W/m²K (convection + radiation); pad top from radiation in = convection (h 4) + conduction through 0.6 m RCC + ground (1.6 m²K/W).
  • Duct in trench: skin couples to trench air (h 4) and by radiation to the RCC; RCC passes it to air and ground.
  • Materials: module128: k = 0.055 + 0.000145·T (Ceramic fibre module 128 kg/m3, 1430 grade (STL-EF REF-01)); blanket96: k = 0.048 + 0.00016·T (Ceramic fibre blanket 96 kg/m3, 1260 grade (STL-EF REF-02)); blanket64: k = 0.02 + 0.00027·T (Ceramic fibre blanket 64 kg/m3, 1260 grade (typical data, A-03)); casi245: k = 0.062 + 7e-05·T (Calcium silicate board 245 kg/m3 (STL-EF REF-04)); microporous: k = 0.021 + 1.1e-05·T (Microporous board 320 kg/m3 (STL-EF REF-03)); hdboard: k = 0.06 + 0.00012·T (HD ceramic fibre board ~300 kg/m3 (assumed, A-04)); ifb: k = 0.14 + 0.00018·T (Insulating firebrick IS 8, ~800 kg/m3 (typical)); concrete: k = 1.7 + 0.0·T (RCC); soil: k = 1.2 + 0.0·T (Soil, moist (assumed)); fibreboard: k = 0.045 + 0.00012·T (Ceramic fibre board 260 kg/m3 (typical))
