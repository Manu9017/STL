# Due diligence: findings from the current sites (Sept 2026)

1. **Leads are leaking.** The enquiry mailto links on all group sites also send to clquery@indianbusinesshub.com. Remove this, and change the hosting and domain credentials if they are held by the vendor.
2. **SEO equity.** There are about 27 indexed old URLs. Map every one with a 301 redirect (see `redirects.csv`).
3. **Brands compete with each other in search:**
   - shivasasia.com (Reinplast) also has an HDG plant page.
   - pptanks.com is branded "Shivas Technology LTD" with a different address (C-71/8, Bulandshahr Road).
   - Decide which site owns which keywords, then cross-link them.
4. **Wrong or outdated claims on the current site:**
   - BS 729 is withdrawn. Replace it with ISO 1461, ASTM A123 and IS 2629 / IS 4759.
   - "100% environmentally friendly" cannot be supported.
   - "Compressive, flexural and impact strength" testing does not apply to machinery.
   - The copyright line says 2019.
5. **Exports to the EU.** From 20 January 2027, machinery supplied into the EU falls under Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230. Show CE marking only if you hold it.
6. **Legal.** The footer needs CIN and GSTIN. Add a privacy policy under the DPDP Act 2023 and cookie consent if analytics tracks EU visitors.
7. **Trust assets to produce:**
   - A factory and site photo and video shoot (including drone).
   - Case studies, with each client's permission.
   - Downloadable datasheets.
   - A 3D plant walkthrough.
8. **Operations.**
   - Use your own-domain email with SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
   - Set up Google Business Profile, Search Console and analytics.
   - Route enquiries into a CRM or sheet.
