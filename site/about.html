Engineering for pickling, galvanizing and wire, since 1991

Company profile

Who we are

SHIVAS began in 1991 as SHIVAS Reinplast Company of India, making acid-proof tanks and fume scrubbers in polypropylene and FRP for pickling and chemical plants. That work put us inside our customers' pretreatment lines, and the group grew with them.

SHIVAS Products India, established in 2002, makes FRP pultruded profiles on its own pultrusion machine and supplies isophthalic and vinyl ester resins. SHIVAS Technology Limited, incorporated in 2019, designs and builds complete hot-dip galvanizing plants and wire drawing machines, with the kettles, furnaces, burners and fume systems in them.

Engineering, fabrication and site work are done by our own team. Plastic, FRP and steel equipment is made in our shops in Ghaziabad; proven bought-out parts, such as burners, drives, pumps and instruments, are built into our systems.
