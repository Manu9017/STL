Careers at SHIVAS

Join our team

We design, make and install galvanizing plants, wire drawing machines, acid-proof tanks and fume scrubbers, from our works in Ghaziabad. If you like seeing your work built in our own shops and running at a customer's plant, we would like to hear from you.

Where we hire

These are the kinds of work we do. Openings change through the year, so send your details even if no job is advertised.

  • Design and draftingMechanical design, 2D drawings and 3D models of plants, machines and tanks.
  • Project and site engineeringErection, commissioning and start-up at customer sites in India and abroad.
  • FabricationMS fabricators and welders, PP and PP-H plastic welders, FRP laminators.
  • Electrical, PLC and automationPanels, drives, burner controls and PLC programming.
  • Sales and proposalsUnderstanding a customer's process and turning it into a scheme and an offer.
  • Purchase, stores and accountsBuying, inspection of bought-out items, dispatch and office work.
  • Apprentices and traineesITI, diploma and engineering graduates who want to learn on real plants.

How it works

  1. Send your CV to info@shivastechnology.com with the area of work in the subject.
  2. If your profile fits a current or coming need, we call you.
  3. Shortlisted people meet us at the works in Ghaziabad.
Please note: SHIVAS never asks for money at any stage of hiring. If anyone asks you to pay for a job with us, do not pay.
