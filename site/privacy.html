Privacy

What this website records when you visit it or write to us, and why.

Who we are

SHIVAS Reinplast Company of India, SHIVAS Technology Limited and SHIVAS Products India, A-1/270, Swadeshi Compound, Kavi Nagar Industrial Area, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh 201002, India. Write to info@shivastechnology.com about anything on this page.

Every visit

The page you open, when, for how long and how far you scroll; whether you use a phone, tablet or computer, its operating system, browser and screen size; your language and time zone; and the website or search engine that sent you here. This does not tell us who you are. The 3D model pages are counted the same way; they do not show the question below, so the choice you made on the rest of the site applies there.

Only if you press OK

Your IP address and what it shows: your approximate city and country and the name of the network you use. On an office connection that is often your company's name. The lookup is done by ipapi.co (or ipwho.is if that is busy). Your browser also keeps a random visitor number, so we can tell a second visit from a first. Google Analytics, if we use it, also runs only after OK.

What you send us

When you use the enquiry form or ask our engineer to check a calculation, we receive what you type (name, company, phone, email, message) and the calculation, together with the pages you looked at if you pressed OK.

Why, and who sees it

To reply to you, to follow up interest in our products, and to improve this website. The records are kept in SHIVAS's Google account and enquiries arrive by email. We do not sell them or give them to anyone else.

How long

Visit records are deleted after 24 months. Enquiries are kept as business records for as long as we deal with you.

Your choice

Your current choice is shown here when the visit log is switched on.

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, write to info@shivastechnology.com or call +91 98109 20468.
