Wire drawing calculator

Rod size to finished size: how many dies, the diameter after each die, the speed of every block, the drawing force and motor power per block, and what the machine makes in a day.

What you enter

  • Material, one of: Low carbon steel (MS, ≤ 0.1 % C); Medium carbon (0.3–0.45 % C); High carbon (0.6–0.85 % C, spring, LRPC); Stainless steel 304
  • Rod / entry Ø, typical 5.5 mm
  • Finished Ø, typical 2 mm
  • Finishing speed, typical 12 m/s
  • Running hours, typical 20 h/day

Under Advanced, 5 more inputs for a closer answer, each set to a typical value until you change it: Largest area reduction per die 25 %; Die half-angle 6 °; Friction in the die 0.05 μ; Drive efficiency 88 %; Utilisation 80 %.

What it works out

  • Dies
  • Total reduction
  • Output
  • Drawing power
  • Finished wire tensile (estimate)
  • Wire weight
  • Rod speed into the first die
  • Installed motors (sum)
  • Die by die: diameter, speed, pull and motor, as a table

How it works

Siebel drawing stress with work hardening per die; back tension, die wear and speed effects are not included. The last die takes a lighter reduction for size and finish. SHIVAS designs the actual die sequence with your rod and wire specification.

Each size it picks (a motor, burner, rectifier or fan) is the next standard size above the calculated need. Choose an extra margin of 10, 20 or 30 % and it is added to the need before the size is picked; nothing else is added.

The answer is a first sizing from typical values, and every input can be changed. Press Ask our engineer to check this under the results and SHIVAS replies with a checked sizing and a proposal.

  • Wet wire drawing machineWet wire drawing machines for fine sizes, with cones running in lubricant.
  • Inverted vertical wire drawing machineInverted vertical wire drawing machines for large coils of heavy wire.
  • Bull block machineBull block machines for heavy sizes, drawing in one or two passes onto a large block.
  • Complete wire drawing lineA complete wire drawing line: payoff, descaling, pointing and butt welding, drawing, and take-up, designed as one.
  • CO₂ / MIG wire plantCO₂ / MIG welding wire plants: drawing, cleaning, copper coating, precision drawing and layer winding.
  • Rotating die boxRotating die boxes that turn the die slowly while drawing, so wear spreads evenly round the bore.
