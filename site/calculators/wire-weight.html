Wire weight and length calculator

From the wire size: the weight per metre and per kilometre, how many metres a coil or spool holds and how long it takes to fill, what the machine makes in an hour and a day, and the zinc a galvanized coating puts on each tonne of wire.

What you enter

  • Wire Ø, typical 2.5 mm
  • Coil or spool weight, typical 1000 kg
  • Line speed, typical 10 m/s
  • Zinc coating, if galvanized (0 = black wire)

Under Advanced, 4 more inputs for a closer answer, each set to a typical value until you change it: Strands or machines running 1; Running hours 20 h/day; Utilisation 80 %; Steel density 7850 kg/m³.

What it works out

  • Weight
  • One coil holds
  • Output
  • Coil fills in
  • Metres per kg
  • Cross-section
  • Output a day
  • Surface per tonne

How it works

Weight from the nominal size at 7,850 kg/m³. Surface per tonne = 4,000 ÷ (7.85 × Ø in mm). Coating thickness at 7.14 g/m² per µm of zinc; zinc lost to dross, ash and wiping comes on top. Check the coating mass against the standard on your order, for example IS 4826 or ISO 7989-2.

The answer is a first sizing from typical values, and every input can be changed. Press Ask our engineer to check this under the results and SHIVAS replies with a checked sizing and a proposal.
