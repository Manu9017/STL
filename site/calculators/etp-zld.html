ETP and ZLD plant calculator

Size the whole effluent plant for a pickling, galvanizing or wire shop, from the acidic effluent to dry salt: the neutraliser and the filter cake, the tanks and clarifier, how much water reverse osmosis gives back, the evaporator (multiple-effect or MVR) with its steam or power, the crystallizer if you want one, and the agitated thin film dryer (ATFD) that turns the last brine into dry salt.

What you enter

  • Dilute effluent: rinses, scrubber bleed, floor wash, typical 88 m³/day
  • Free acid in it, typical 1.5 g/L HCl
  • Iron in it, typical 0.8 g/L
  • Spent acid neutralised in the ETP, typical 2 m³/day
  • Neutraliser, one of: Hydrated lime; Caustic soda; Lime, then soda ash
  • Evaporation and drying, one of: MEE, then ATFD; MEE with crystallizer, then ATFD; MVR, then ATFD; MVR with crystallizer, then ATFD; No evaporator: ATFD takes the RO reject
  • Effects in the MEE, one of: 3 effects; 4 effects; 5 effects

Under Advanced, 24 more inputs for a closer answer, each set to a typical value until you change it: Iron in the spent acid 130 g/L; Free acid in the spent acid 3 % HCl; Density of the spent acid 1.25 kg/L; Zinc in the spent acid 0 g/L; Other dissolved salts in the dilute effluent 1500 mg/L; ETP running hours 20 h/day; Lime purity 90 % Ca(OH)₂; Reagent excess 10 %; Solids in the filter cake 35 %; Equalisation, hours of average flow 12 h; Clarifier surface loading 1 m³/m²·h; Oxygen transfer of the aeration 10 %; RO recovery wanted 75 %; Highest salt in the RO reject 60,000 mg/L; RO salt rejection 98 %; MEE steam economy typical for the effects; MVR power per m³ evaporated 30 kWh; Solids in the evaporator concentrate near saturation; Salt the crystallizer takes out 85 %; Dryness of the ATFD product 95 % solids; ATFD steam per kg of water 1.25 kg/kg; Evaporator and ATFD running hours 24 h/day; Steam price 2500 ₹/t; Power price 8 ₹/kWh.

How it works

Neutraliser from the chloride the acid carries: 1.02 kg of lime per kg of HCl and 1.33 kg per kg of iron, or 1.10 and 1.43 kg of caustic soda; aeration turns the iron into Fe(OH)₃. RO recovery is the lower of what you ask and what keeps the reject under your limit; feed pressure is estimated from the reject strength. Evaporation uses typical steam economies of 2.5, 3.2 and 3.9 for 3, 4 and 5 effects, or the MVR power you set, and the ATFD dries the last brine to a powder. The options table runs the same sizing for each evaporator on your inputs. ETP pumps, agitators and blowers are not included. A first sizing for budgets and layouts: SHIVAS Reinplast confirms it on your effluent analysis, with jar and pilot tests.

The answer is a first sizing from typical values, and every input can be changed. Press Ask our engineer to check this under the results and SHIVAS replies with a checked sizing and a proposal.
