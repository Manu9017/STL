Fume scrubber and blower calculator

Size a packed-bed fume scrubber from your tanks: the extraction air, tower diameter and packing height, recirculation pump, pressure drop, and the blower motor in kW and HP.

What you enter

  • Fume, one of: HCl fume (pickling); H₂SO₄ mist; HF / HNO₃ / NOx (stainless pickling); Ammonia / flux fume; Chromic acid mist (plating)
  • Tanks extracted, typical 3
  • Tank length, typical 6 m
  • Tank width, typical 1.2 m

Under Advanced, 7 more inputs for a closer answer, each set to a typical value until you change it: Capture rate 0.38 m³/s·m²; Extra air 0 m³/h; Tower air velocity 2 m/s; Removal wanted 95 %; Liquid / gas ratio 2.5 L/m³; Duct run 40 m; Fan efficiency 62 %.

What it works out

  • Air flow
  • Tower
  • Blower motor
  • Recirculation
  • System pressure drop
  • Transfer units needed
  • Main duct at 12 m/s
  • Scrubbing liquid
  • Recommended blower
  • HCl evaporated (≈ 50 g/m²·h)

How it works

Capture air from tank surface area × capture rate; packing height = transfer units × height of a transfer unit for random PP packing. Pressure drop scales with the square of tower velocity. SHIVAS Reinplast builds the hoods, ducts, scrubber, FRP blower and stack in PP / FRP.

Each size it picks (a motor, burner, rectifier or fan) is the next standard size above the calculated need. Choose an extra margin of 10, 20 or 30 % and it is added to the need before the size is picked; nothing else is added.

The answer is a first sizing from typical values, and every input can be changed. Press Ask our engineer to check this under the results and SHIVAS replies with a checked sizing and a proposal.
