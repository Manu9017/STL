Pickling acid calculator

How much hydrochloric acid your pickling uses, how much spent acid you have to dispose of or regenerate each month, and what one tank charge holds.

What you enter

  • Steel pickled, typical 1500 t/mo
  • Pickling tanks, typical 3
  • Working volume per tank, typical 22 m³

Under Advanced, 3 more inputs for a closer answer, each set to a typical value until you change it: Iron + scale removed 0.8 % of steel; Iron at which the bath is spent 130 g/L; Bath strength when made up 15 % HCl.

What it works out

  • Acid 33 %
  • Spent acid
  • Iron dissolved
  • One tank charge
  • Tank volume, all tanks
  • Bath life (all tanks)
  • Pure HCl consumed
  • Regeneration

How it works

Fe + 2HCl → FeCl₂ + H₂; about 85 % of the acid charged is used before the bath is spent. Real use depends on the scale, inhibitor, temperature and drag-out. SHIVAS Reinplast builds pickling lines, fume scrubbers, acid recovery and effluent plants.

The answer is a first sizing from typical values, and every input can be changed. Press Ask our engineer to check this under the results and SHIVAS replies with a checked sizing and a proposal.
