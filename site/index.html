What we do

Four kinds of work. Pick the one you need.

SHIVAS Technology Limited

Galvanizing plants

Steel is cleaned and dipped in molten zinc at about 450 °C, so it does not rust for decades. We build the complete plant, or the part you need: kettle, furnace, cleaning tanks, fume extraction, cranes and controls.

  • Turnkey galvanizing plantThe complete plant: study, design, supply, erection and start-up.
  • Zinc kettleThe steel bath that holds the molten zinc at about 450 °C.
  • Galvanizing furnaceHeats the zinc kettle evenly, with burners firing along its walls.
  • Zinc fume extractionDraws the fume off the zinc bath and filters it through bags.

SHIVAS Technology Limited

Wire drawing machines

Thick steel rod is pulled through a series of dies until it becomes wire of the size you need. We build the drawing machines and everything around them, from the coil payoff to the finished spool.

  • Straight line wire drawing machinePulls rod through a row of dies in a straight line, without twisting it.
  • OTO type wire drawing machineDraws wire block to block, keeping a few turns on each block as a buffer.
  • Dead block coilerWinds finished wire into large, tight coils while the line keeps running.
  • Complete wire drawing lineA complete line from rod coil to finished wire.

SHIVAS Reinplast Company of India

Acid tanks, scrubbers and effluent plants

Steel is cleaned in strong acid before it is galvanized or drawn. SHIVAS Reinplast makes the plastic and fibreglass tanks that hold the acid, the scrubbers that clean its fume, and the plants that treat the waste water.

  • PP & PP-H process tanksPlastic tanks for acid, cleaning and rinse baths.
  • Fume scrubbers & extractionWashes acid fume out of the air before it leaves the stack.
  • Pickling plantsComplete lines that clean rust and scale off steel with acid.
  • Effluent treatment plantsCleans the waste water from pickling and galvanizing.

How we work

From the first talk to the first dip

One team takes the project from study to start-up, and stays for spares and support afterwards.

  1. StudyYour products, tonnage and site; layout, zinc and fuel cost, and the return on the plant.
  2. EngineerProcess design, drawings, burners and the control system.
  3. FabricateTanks, ducts and steelwork made in our own shops; bought-out items to our specification.
  4. ErectOur crew installs the furnace, kettle, tanks, ducting and controls on your site.
  5. CommissionFurnace dry-out, kettle fill, the first dip and training for your operators.
  6. SupportSpares, kettle changes, upgrades and process audits for the life of the plant.
