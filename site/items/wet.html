Overview

For fine wire the dies and stepped cones run inside a bath of liquid lubricant, which cools the wire and dies and carries away fines. The wire passes many dies in one compact machine.

SHIVAS wet drawing machines are built for fine wire production with take-up capstans and spoolers.
