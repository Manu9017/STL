Overview

Dross builds up on the kettle floor and must be removed regularly. A dross grab lowered by the crane closes on the settled dross and lifts it out with little zinc. A zinc pump moves molten zinc for kettle changes or transfers.

SHIVAS supplies grabs and pumps matched to your kettle depth and crane, with moulds for dross and zinc.
