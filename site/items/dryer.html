Overview

Wet flux film on cold steel boils violently in molten zinc, throwing zinc out of the kettle and creating ash. Pre-drying the work after flux removes the water and warms the steel, so the zinc does less work and the coating forms evenly.

SHIVAS dryers are pits or tunnels with covers, fed by recovered furnace flue gas and a top-up burner, with a circulation fan to move air evenly round the load.

How it works

Recirculating dryer · 4 positions

Watch it run →

Loads index through four positions in a closed chamber. Entry and exit doors open only while the line moves, and a fan recirculates hot air through the loads, heated by furnace flue gas with a burner top-up.

Main parts

  1. 1
    Entry doorOpens to admit the next load, then closes to hold the heat in.
  2. 2
    Positions P1–P4Each load dries in four steps, wettest at P1.
  3. 3
    Recirculation fanMoves hot air up through the loads and back.
  4. 4
    Flue gas heat exchangerFurnace flue heat, with a top-up burner when needed.
  5. 5
    Hot airRises through the work and returns to the fan.
  6. 6
    Exit doorReleases the driest load towards the kettle.
