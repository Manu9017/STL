Overview

Dipping cranes work hard: dozens of cycles a shift, loads lowered slowly into the zinc and lifted out at a controlled speed for good drainage. Over the acid line they also face corrosive air.

SHIVAS specifies the crane capacity, span, lift and speeds from the dip cycle and the jig load, and selects the protection the area needs. Cranes come from specialist makers and are integrated into the plant layout and controls.
