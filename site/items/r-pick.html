Overview

Pickling removes scale with acid, usually hydrochloric. A good plant controls acid strength, iron content and temperature so pickling is fast and acid use is low, and keeps the fume inside the equipment.

SHIVAS Reinplast designs strip pickling lines, fumeless multi-strand wire pickling and batch pickling for pipes, tubes and structures, with tanks, hoods, scrubbers, acid recovery and effluent treatment.

How it works

Push-pull pickling line

Watch it run →

Each coil is threaded on its own: entry pinch rolls push the strip head through the acid tanks until the recoiler grips it and pulls the coil through at speed. No welder and no loopers, so it suits mixed widths and gauges.

  3. 3

Continuous pickling line

Watch it run →

Coil ends are welded together so strip never stops in the acid. An entry looper stores strip while the entry section halts for each weld, and an exit looper does the same at the coiler, so the process section runs at constant speed.

Main parts

  1. 1
    UncoilersOne runs while the next coil is prepared.
  2. 2
    Shear + welderCrops and welds the new coil to the old tail.
  3. 3
    Entry looperStores strip so the process never stops for a weld.
  4. 4
    Scale breakerTension leveller cracks scale before the acid.
  5. 5
    Pickling tanksLong turbulent HCl tanks.
  6. 6
    Rinse + dryerCascade rinse and hot-air drying.
  7. 7
    Exit looperBuffers strip during coil change at the exit.
  8. 8
    RecoilerCoils the finished strip.

Fumeless wire pickling

Watch it run →

Wire runs as up to 50 parallel strands through fully covered acid, rinse and coating tanks. Covers and extraction keep the fume inside the line, so the shop stays clear. Set strands, speed and size to see the throughput.

Tunnel pickling

Watch it run →

The whole pretreatment line sits inside a closed tunnel. An internal hoist carries each jig from tank to tank; roller doors open only to let a jig in or out. Roof hoods pull the fume to a scrubber, fresh air enters low through louvres, and the shop floor stays clear of acid mist.

Shown running as: Running · Fume view

  1. 1

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

  6. 6

  8. 8

  9. 9

Closed-loop pickling

Watch it run →

Every waste stream from a pickling or galvanizing shop has somewhere to go. Spent acid goes to regeneration and comes back as acid, rinse water goes through the ETP and ZLD and comes back as water, fume is scrubbed, and what is left leaves as dry salt and filter cake.

Main parts

  1. 1
    Pickling tanksPP-H tanks with lip extraction.
  2. 2
    Fume scrubberScrubs the extracted fume; bleed goes to the ETP.
  3. 3
    Acid regenerationSpent acid back to fresh acid plus iron oxide.
  4. 4
    ETPNeutralise, settle, filter.
  5. 5
    ZLDRO, evaporator and ATFD.
  6. 6
    Water backPermeate and condensate return to the rinses.
  7. 7
    Solids outIron oxide, filter cake and dry salt.
