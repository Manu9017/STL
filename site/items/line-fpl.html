Overview

Fumeless pickling runs strands through covered tanks with controlled acid and temperature, so fume stays in the equipment and is drawn off to a small scrubber.

Tank lids and strand slots are designed so very little air enters, which keeps the extraction small. Acid strength, temperature and iron are held in a narrow band; rinsing is cascaded; the small scrubber stream can go to acid recovery.

Every stage of the line

In order from the coil going in to the coil coming out. Watch it run in the wire lines section.

  1. Pay-off
  2. 1
    Rod pay-offsTechnology

    Overhead or rotating pay-offs feed hot-rolled rod. Butt welding joins each new coil to the last, so strands never need re-threading.

    Feed
    Hot-rolled rod coils
    Joining
    Butt welding
    Built by
    SHIVAS Technology
  3. Pretreatment
  4. 2
    Mechanical descalerTechnology

    Reverse-bending rolls crack the mill scale and brushes take it off. Most of the scale leaves dry, so the acid lasts much longer.

    Method
    Reverse bending + brushing
    Scale
    Collected dry
    Gain
    Less acid, less iron load
  5. 3
    Fumeless HCl picklingReinplast

    Two covered tanks in series. The rod meets the older acid first and the fresh acid last, while the acid cascades the other way, so it’s used up before it goes to regeneration. Lids, water seals and slot extraction keep the fume in the tank.

    Chemistry
    HCl 12–18 % + inhibitor
    Temperature
    Ambient to 35 °C
    Flow
    Counter-current cascade
    Build
    PP-H tanks, FRP lids, PP pumps
  6. 4
    High-pressure spray rinseReinplast

    Jets strip loose smut and acid from the rod surface before the rinse tanks.

    Nozzles
    High-pressure, ring headers
    Water
    Recirculated
    Build
    PP-H spray box
  7. 5
    Cascade rinseReinplast

    Two or three counter-flow stages with a spray at the exit. Clean water enters only at the last stage, so water use stays low and the overflow goes to the ETP.

    Stages
    2–3, counter-flow
    Overflow
    To ETP or acid make-up
    Build
    PP-H tanks, PP spray headers
  8. Coating
  9. 6
    Carrier coatingReinplast

    A zinc phosphate, borax or lime carrier holds the drawing soap in the die and protects the rod until it is drawn.

    Chemistry
    Zinc phosphate, borax or lime
    Temperature
    ≈ 60–90 °C
    Sludge
    Filter loop on the tank
    Build
    PP-H tank, PTFE heating
  10. Post-treatment
  11. 7
    DryerTechnology

    Hot air sets the coating so it doesn’t smear in the soap box.

    Temperature
    ≈ 120–180 °C
    Build
    Insulated box, hot-air blower
  12. Drawing
  13. 8
    Inline drawingTechnologyOption: Inline drawing

    Machine
    Straight-line, multi-block
    Lubricant
    Soap in rotating die boxes
    Cooling

    Built by

  15. 9
    CoilersTechnology

    A coiler for each strand, inverted vertical or dead block, building tight, heavy coils on carriers while the line keeps running.

    Package

    Type

Around the line

  • Fume scrubber + FRP blower ReinplastEvery covered tank is extracted to a packed-bed scrubber, FRP blower and chimney.
  • Acid regeneration ReinplastSpent acid goes back as fresh HCl and iron oxide.
  • Phosphate sludge filter ReinplastKeeps the carrier bath clean between cleanouts.
  • ETP + ZLD ReinplastRinse overflow is treated and reused.
