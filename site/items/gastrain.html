Dual fuel & gas train

Gas and oil trains that deliver fuel to the burners safely, at the right pressure, with proving before every start.

  • Fuels: NG / LPG · LDO / FO
  • Safety: Double shut-off + proving
  • Changeover: Purge, then switch

Overview

Every burner needs a fuel train: isolation, filtering, pressure regulation, two automatic shut-off valves, pressure switches and gauges, and on larger burners valve proving. On oil, a pump set, filter, heater for heavy oil and solenoid valves do the same job.

SHIVAS lays out the trains on a common skid, sized for the furnace rating, and ties them into the burner management system so a fault shuts fuel off and holds it off until reset.
