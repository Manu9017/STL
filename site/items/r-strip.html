Overview

Hot-rolled strip is uncoiled, pickled in hydrochloric acid, rinsed, dried and recoiled. SHIVAS Reinplast designs the process section: tanks, guides, squeeze rolls, rinses, hoods and scrubbing.

Tank lengths follow strip speed and the pickling time your steel needs; squeeze rolls between tanks limit carry-over, cascade rinsing cuts water use, and the hot air dryer prevents flash rust before recoiling. Tunnel hoods keep fume inside the line and feed a packed scrubber.
