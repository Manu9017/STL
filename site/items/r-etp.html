Overview

Effluent from pickling and galvanizing is acidic and carries iron and zinc. Treatment equalises the flow, neutralises with lime or caustic, precipitates metals, settles them in a clarifier and dewaters the sludge.

SHIVAS Reinplast designs ETPs on your flows and analysis, with PP and FRP tanks and the dosing, clarifier and filter press.

How it works

Effluent treatment plant

Watch it run →

Rinse water and spent solutions are equalised, neutralised, coagulated and settled. Clear water is polished through sand and carbon filters, and sludge leaves as filter-press cake.

Main parts

  1. 1
    EqualisationEvens out flow and strength, with air mixing.
  2. 2
    NeutralisationLime or caustic dosing brings pH to neutral.
  3. 3
    Flash mixerCoagulant dosed and mixed fast.
  4. 4
    FlocculatorSlow mixing grows settleable flocs.
  5. 5
    Tube settlerInclined plates settle the solids; clear water overflows.
  6. 6
    PSF + ACFPressure sand and activated carbon filters polish the water.
  7. 7
    Treated waterReady for reuse or for ZLD.
  8. 8
    Sludge filter pressDewaters sludge into cake.
