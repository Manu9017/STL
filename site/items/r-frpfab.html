Overview

Steel gratings and railings corrode fast in acid shops and coastal sites. FRP versions last without painting.

Moulded gratings give bidirectional strength and a gritted anti-slip top; pultruded gratings span further in one direction. Handrails and fencing are assembled from pultruded tubes and connectors, cut and fitted at site without hot work.
