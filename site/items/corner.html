Overview

Kettle corners see the highest combination of stress and heat, and a weld there is the usual place for zinc attack to start. Bending the corner from the plate puts the welds in the straight walls, where stress is lower and heating can be kept even.

Our press forms heavy kettle plate to a controlled radius. The same process lets us build replacement kettles to match existing furnace dimensions exactly.

How it works

Kettle corner bending

Watch it run →

Kettle corners carry the highest stress and the sharpest temperature changes. We form them on our own press from one plate, so the corner is parent metal and the seams sit in the straight walls.

Shown running as: SHIVAS bent corner · Welded corner

Main parts

  1. 1
    Hydraulic cylinderDrives the ram with controlled force and speed.
  2. 2
    Radius punchSets the inside radius of the corner.
  3. 3
    V dieSupports the plate as it folds to 90°.
  4. 4
    Kettle plateThick kettle-grade steel plate.
  5. 5
    Bent cornerParent metal through the corner. No weld.
  6. 6
    Weld seamMoved into the straight wall, away from corner stress.
