Overview

A dead block coiler takes the wire from the last die on a vertical block and strips it downward into a neat coil on a stand or carrier. It finishes the line with a coil that handles well in storage, pickling or galvanizing.

SHIVAS dead block coilers are matched to the drawing machine speed and the coil weight you need.

How it works

Dead block coiler

Watch it run →

The last block draws the wire and lets each turn slide down onto a stationary block below. Turns stack into a heavy, compact coil on the carrier while the line keeps running.

Main parts

  1. 1
    Final dieSizes the wire to its finished diameter.
  2. 2
    Drawing blockAC-drive block, synchronised with the line, pulls wire through the die.
  3. 3
    Dead blockStationary stem. Turns slide off the drawing block and stack here.
  4. 4
    Coil buildTurns lay down ring on ring into a dense coil.
  5. 5
    CarrierThe full coil leaves on its carrier and an empty one takes its place.
