Overview

Many sites need more than one stage: a cyclone for coarse dust, a bag filter for fine dust, a scrubber for gases. SHIVAS Reinplast designs combined systems for the whole duty.

Stages are sized together so pressure drop, fan duty and collection efficiency are balanced, and so each stage protects the next: the cyclone takes coarse dust off the bags, the bags take dust off the scrubber packing. Sampling ports and differential pressure monitoring are built in.
