Overview

Electro-galvanizing deposits zinc from an acid zinc bath onto the wire by electric current. Coating weight follows the current and time in the cells, so thin, even coats are easy to control.

SHIVAS builds the cleaning, plating cells, rectifiers, rinsing and drying for multi-strand lines. The coating thickness calculator on our wire page shows the relationship.

How it works

Electro-galvanizing line

Watch it run →

Wire runs through cleaning, pickling and long electrolytic cells where zinc leaves the anodes and plates onto the wire, which is the cathode. Coating weight follows current and time in the cell, so it can be set closely.

Main parts

  1. 1
    PayoffsOne coil per strand.
  2. 2
    Electrolytic degreasingCleans the surface with current.
  3. 3
    PicklingRemoves oxide before plating.
  4. 4
    Zinc anodesDissolve into the electrolyte as Zn²⁺.
  5. 5
    Wire as cathodeContact rollers make the wire negative; zinc plates onto it.
  6. 6
    RectifierDC current sets the coating weight.
  7. 7
    Passivation + dryerProtects the fresh zinc.
  8. 8
    Take-upCoilers or spoolers.
