Overview

A galvanizing plant produces spent pickling acid loaded with iron and zinc, rinse water, scrubber bleed and sludge. Handled badly, they are a cost and a legal risk. Handled well, acid and water go back into the process.

SHIVAS Reinplast designs physico-chemical ETPs, acid recovery and regeneration, and ZLD systems with evaporators and dryers, sized on your actual streams.
