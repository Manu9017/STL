Overview

Fresh galvanized steel leaves the kettle at about 450 °C. Quenching in water stops further zinc-iron alloy growth, brightens the finish and makes the work safe to handle sooner. Passivation then leaves a thin protective film that reduces white rust when work is stored or stacked wet.

SHIVAS sizes quench and passivation tanks for your loads. The heat the work brings into the quench is not wasted: hot quench water is pumped through the flux tank heat exchanger, which warms the flux and cools the quench at the same time.
