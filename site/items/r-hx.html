Overview

Heating pickling acid or cooling corrosive liquors needs an exchanger that survives the chemical. Impregnated graphite conducts heat well and resists hydrochloric and sulphuric acids.

SHIVAS Reinplast supplies graphite exchangers sized for your heat duty.
