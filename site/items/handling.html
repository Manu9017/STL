Overview

In a busy galvanizing plant the crane is often the real bottleneck, not the kettle. Material handling is planned so black steel is jigged in one bay, travels through pretreatment and the kettle on one route, and is unjigged and dispatched without crossing the incoming flow.

SHIVAS lays out jigging bays, transfer cars between bays, crane runways and jig stands to the dip cycle, and specifies the cranes and hoists to suit.
