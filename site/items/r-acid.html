Overview

Spent pickling acid contains free acid and iron chloride. Recovery systems separate free acid for reuse; regeneration plants such as spray roasters convert iron chloride back into hydrochloric acid and iron oxide.

SHIVAS Reinplast sizes acid systems on your spent acid volume and analysis.

How it works

Acid regeneration

Watch it run →

Spent pickle liquor is concentrated in a venturi, sprayed into a fired roaster and split into iron oxide and HCl gas. The gas is absorbed in rinse water to make regenerated acid, the oxide is collected as a by-product, and the off-gas is scrubbed before the stack.

Main parts

  1. 1
    Spent acid tankFeCl₂ liquor from the pickling line.
  2. 2
    Venturi pre-concentratorHot roaster gas evaporates water from the feed.
  3. 3
    Spray roasterFired roaster: FeCl₂ + H₂O + O₂ → Fe₂O₃ + HCl.
  4. 4
    BurnersTangential burners heat the roaster.
  5. 5
    CycloneReturns carried-over oxide to the roaster.
  6. 6
    Absorber columnRinse water absorbs HCl to regenerated acid.
  7. 7
    Off-gas scrubber + stackFinal scrub before the stack.
  8. 8
    Iron oxideCollected and bagged as a by-product.
  9. 9
    Regenerated acidBack to the pickling line.
