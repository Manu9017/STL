Overview

An electric furnace hangs resistance elements on the furnace walls facing the kettle. Each zone has its own thermocouple and thyristor controller, so the wall is heated evenly at a low flux, which is kind to the kettle and gives tight zinc temperature control.

Where electricity is reliable and fairly priced, or where combustion and flue emissions are a problem, electric heating is simple to run and maintain. The insulated lid keeps idle losses low.

How it works

Electric kettle furnace

Watch it run →

No burners and no flue at the kettle. Resistance elements line the furnace walls in eight independently controlled zones. Thyristor controllers trim each zone to its own thermocouple, so the wall flux stays low and even and the kettle lasts.

Shown running as: Heat-up · Hold · PID · Lid closed
