Overview

Zinc ash skimmed from the bath surface contains a large share of metallic zinc trapped in oxide and chloride. Sold as ash it fetches a fraction of the zinc price. A recovery unit separates the metal so it can be cast and returned to the kettle.

SHIVAS offers recovery units from compact manual units to semi-automatic rotary plants, with fume capture and casting.
