Overview

Odours from effluent plants and processes are captured from covered sources and treated in chemical scrubbers or biofilters before discharge.

Odour sources are covered first, because treating a small, concentrated air stream is far cheaper than treating a whole building. The treatment stage is then chosen on the compounds present: acid or alkaline scrubbing for ammonia and sulphides, biofiltration for biodegradable organics.
