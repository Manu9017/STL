Overview

In an OTO machine the wire is stored as turns on each block and leaves over the top through a guide pulley to the next die. The stored turns act as a buffer, so blocks do not have to be in perfect step.

SHIVAS OTO machines suit low-carbon and MS wire where flexibility and simple maintenance matter more than avoiding twist.

How it works

OTO type wire drawing machine

Watch it run →

Each block stores a few dozen turns of wire. The wire leaves over the top through a flyer, runs up over one overhead pulley and down into the next die. The stored turns soak up small speed differences between blocks, so no tension control is needed. Simple and forgiving; the over-the-top take-off puts a twist in the wire, so it suits mild steel and binding wire.

Questions buyers ask

Does OTO twist the wire?

Each turn over the top adds a twist, which matters for high-carbon and spring wire but not for most low-carbon products.
