Overview

The payoff sets the rhythm of the whole line: if a coil snags, everything stops. Overhead payoffs let rod rise off a stationary coil through a guide ring; rotating payoffs turn the coil for wire that must not twist.

SHIVAS supplies payoffs for rod and wire, sized for coil weight and speed, often paired with a butt welder so the next coil is joined without stopping.
