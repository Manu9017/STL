Overview

A fixed die wears unevenly, producing oval wire. Turning the die slowly while drawing spreads the wear, so the die keeps its size and roundness longer.

SHIVAS rotating die boxes include the soap box, die holder, drive and cooling.

How it works

Rotating die box

Watch it run →

The die turns slowly in its holder while wire passes through, so wear spreads evenly round the bore. Agitated soap feeds lubricant into the die and a water jacket carries the heat away.

Main parts

  1. 1
    Soap boxDry drawing soap packed round the wire entry.
  2. 2
    AgitatorKeeps soap loose so it coats the wire instead of tunnelling.
  3. 3
    Die nibCarbide die that sizes the wire.
  4. 4
    Drive gearWorm and gear turn the die holder slowly.
  5. 5
    Water jacketCooling water round the die holder.
