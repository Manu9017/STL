Overview

When fluxed steel enters the zinc, the flux decomposes and gives off dense white fume of zinc and ammonium chlorides. An enclosure round the kettle contains it so the extraction can pull it away at a reasonable air volume.

SHIVAS builds fixed enclosures with sliding doors or panels, and travelling enclosures that move with the crane, each designed round your kettle, crane and load size.
