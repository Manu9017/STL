Overview

ZLD takes treated effluent, concentrates it in multiple-effect or mechanical vapour recompression evaporators, and dries the concentrate in an agitated thin-film dryer. Condensate is reused; salts leave as solids.

SHIVAS Reinplast integrates ZLD with ETP and acid recovery so the evaporator load is as small as possible.

How it works

ZLD · RO · MEE / MVR · ATFD

Watch it run →

Treated effluent is concentrated until no liquid leaves the site. RO recovers most of the water, an evaporator concentrates the reject, and an agitated thin film dryer turns what is left into dry salt.

Shown running as: MEE · MVR

Main parts

  1. 1
    ROMembranes recover clean permeate from treated effluent.
  2. 2
    PermeateBack to the process.
  3. 3
    MEE effectsThree evaporators in series. Vapour from each heats the next.
  4. 4
    Live steamOnly the first effect takes fresh steam.
  5. 5
    CondenserLast-effect vapour condensed and recovered.
  6. 6
    ATFDRotor spreads concentrate thin on a steam-heated wall and dries it.
  7. 7
    Dry saltBagged solids. No liquid leaves.
