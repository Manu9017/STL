Overview

Hydrochloric acid fume rises from warm pickling tanks and spreads through the shed if it is not captured. Lip extraction draws air across the tank surface through slots along the rim, catching fume where it is released. For acid enclosed rooms, room extraction keeps the space under negative pressure.

The fume-laden air goes through corrosion-proof ducting to a packed-bed scrubber, where a recirculating liquor absorbs the acid, then through a mist eliminator, an FRP blower and the stack. SHIVAS Reinplast fabricates the hoods, ducts, scrubber and blower.

Questions buyers ask

Lip or room extraction?

Lip extraction needs less air for open lines; room extraction suits an acid enclosed room. Many plants combine both.
